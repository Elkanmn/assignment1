About Espresso

Espresso is a small but very strong coffee drink. It is made when hot water goes through ground coffee under high pressure. One cup of espresso is only about 30 millilitres, so people usually drink it very quickly. Espresso is also the base for many other drinks, for example cappuccino and latte. I chose this drink because it is simple and classic.

Key Dates

  1. 1884 - Angelo Moriondo patented the first steam coffee machine in Turin
  2. 1901 - Luigi Bezzera improved the machine and got a new patent
  3. 1903 - Desiderio Pavoni bought the patent and started production
  4. 1938 - Achille Gaggia worked on a lever machine
  5. 1948 - Gaggia started to sell lever machines that made crema
  6. 1961 - The Faema E61 machine used a pump instead of a lever

Interesting Facts

  • One shot of espresso is about 30 ml
  • The brown foam on top is called crema
  • Espresso has less caffeine per cup than a big americano
  • The water pressure in the machine is about 9 bar
  • The word "espresso" means "fast" in Italian
